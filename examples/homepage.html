APERTUS

Apertus is a flagship project of the Swiss AI Initiative

Fully Open Foundation Model for Sovereign AI

Bilingual model family built and released in the open, together with a coalition of research institutions — trained on one of the world's leading AI supercomputers in collaboration with the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre.

Ethics & Governance

Trustworthy use, by construction

Transparency work happens before deployment, not after: artifact cards document what was released, how it was trained, and under which licenses. Stakeholders — from public institutions to SMEs and start-ups — can audit and adopt without guesswork.
